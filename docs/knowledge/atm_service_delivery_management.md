###  FF-ICE/R1 RouteTrajectory

ICAO Doc 9965 Ed. 2 Volume I, the FF-ICE Concept document, explains at high-level how FF-ICE supports TBO including trajectory synchronisation activities and focuses on 4D trajectory exchanges between ATM participants. In this context, it only speaks of desired, negotiating and agreed 4D trajectories. ICAO Doc 9965 Ed. 2 Volume II, the implementation guidance manual for the first Release of FF-ICE, acknowledges that as a minimum, eASPs should support the exchange of desired/negotiating/agreed route without trajectory points, to allow for easy initial transition by operators. The notions of desired, negotiating and agreed RouteTrajectoryGroups supports not only desired, negotiating and agreed 4D trajectories but also desired, negotiating and agreed routes. This diagram brings together the operational language from both Volume I (the concept) and Volume II (the means to realise the concept).
