###  Airborne Events - Over Point

The diagram describes the OverPoint that represents the event of flying over a significant point. The diagram highlights the relevant times that are associated with the event, such as the ICAO concept of ETO or the SESAR concept of TTO. The diagram also outlines the related vocabulary that is specific to AMAN operations.
