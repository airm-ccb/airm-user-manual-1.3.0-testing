# How to join the AIRM Community

The `AIRM Community` is the group of AIRM stakeholders that actively contribute to or monitor the evolution of the AIRM. 

## Benefits

As member of the `AIRM Community`, you can
- Follow the latest `AIRM` developments and upcoming updates.
- Propose changes to the `AIRM` and review proposals from other contributors. 
- Can apply for `AIRM CCB` membership

The contributions made are provided on a royalty-free basis.

## Join now!

To become a member of the AIRM Community, simply register to the AIRM Community Area.
